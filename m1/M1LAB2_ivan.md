Assumptions
-All items are on the counter.
-The robot’s gripper is empty.
-The bread bag and jars are closed.

Instructions
1.Place the plate flat on the counter.
2.Remove the twist tie from the bread bag.
3.Open the bread bag.
4.Take the bread loaf.
5.Place the bread loaf on the counter.
6.Pick up the knife.
7.Cut the bread loaf.
8.Place the knife on the counter.
9.Place slice 1 flat on the plate.
10.Open the peanut butter jar.
11.Remove the foil seal from the peanut butter jar.
12.Pick up the knife.
13.Dip the knife in the peanut butter.
14.Spread the peanut butter on slice 1.
15.Place the knife on the counter.
16.Close the peanut butter jar.
17.Open the jelly jar.
18.Remove the foil seal from the jelly jar.
19.Pick up the knife.
20.Dip the knife in the jelly.
22.Spread the jelly on slice 2.
23.Place the knife on the counter.
24.Close the jelly jar.
25.Place slice 2 on slice 1.
Done When:
The sandwich is on the plate with peanut butter and jelly between the bread slices.
